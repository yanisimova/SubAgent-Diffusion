## B. Purpose and context

## C. Three-level finding and independent judgment

1. **ET generally:** 2025 original respondent surveys show widespread self-reported AI use/exposure among developers/technology professionals. The Stack Overflow 84% figure combines **use or intention**, and DORA's 90% concerns AI at work in a different group. Neither establishes a representative company diffusion rate or a Rogers adopter category for this industry's firms.
2. **Application:** No cited original source quantifies routine, successful use for reviewed unit-test/documentation tasks at comparable companies. Rogers attributes: relative advantage mixed/unknown pending baseline; compatibility sensitive to security and CI practices; complexity mixed because verification is nontrivial; trialability favorable under data isolation; observability feasible through measurable quality-adjusted throughput. These are analytic inferences, not observed causal effects.
3. **Organization:** Moderate fast follower: prepare a gated 4–6 week pilot after security approval; conservative: delay proprietary-code trial pending governance/capacity (synthetic examples only meanwhile); aggressive: begin a 2–4 week isolated evaluation promptly if controls are verified. **General evidence stays constant** across all three.

**Strongest signal:** DORA's 2025 reported high AI use at work, corroborated in direction by Stack Overflow's 2025 developer tool survey, despite different questions and populations.

**Key inhibitor:** Trust in output quality and the human verification/security burden; Stack Overflow reported 29% accuracy trust in 2025. Trust responses are not a defect-rate estimate.

**Independent decision:** This example warrants *structured learning about the application*, not an asserted shift to the early majority or an organization-wide adoption decision. The primary organization's review pipeline makes a restricted, reversible test reasonable after baseline and security preparation. Timing follows consequence tolerance and capacity, not hype.

**Confidence:** Moderate for broad surveyed professional use; low for application-specific peer diffusion, adopter category, comparative ROI or the hypothetical firm's expected pilot result.

**Reassessment:** Refresh original survey data within 90 days; obtain actual-use and peer-specific denominator; compare local review-adjusted turnaround, defects, rework, and data incidents with baseline; revisit on model policy, tooling or standards changes.
